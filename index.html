SMS notifications

We use SMS to deliver operational alerts and status notifications from these automation systems to the proprietor's own mobile number. Messages are transactional. We do not send marketing, promotional or bulk messages, and we do not message third parties.

Message frequency varies with system activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for assistance.
